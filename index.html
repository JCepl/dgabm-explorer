Exact weights

A weight says how much a feature counts in the PCA. Every ticked feature is standardized, then multiplied by the square root of its weight, so its share of the variance is proportional to the weight: 1 = normal, 2 = counts double, 0.5 = half, 0 = left out.

Suggest features

Three screening steps on the features of the current scope: (1) prevalence of 0/1 features, (2) redundancy with an already kept feature, (3) communality on the PCs covering 70% of the variance. Each threshold can be changed.

Co-occurrence matrix

Feature clustering (co-occurrence-based)

Clusters the 47 FEATURES by how similar their co-occurrence pattern is (Jaccard distance, average linkage) - not the PCA, which clusters the papers. The dendrogram sits on the left and top edge of the heatmap it orders. A very short branch usually means two near-duplicate features.
